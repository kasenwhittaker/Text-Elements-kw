History

The first garden strawberry was grown in Brittany, France during the late 18th Century. Strawberries were featured on an anime called Death Note with the following quote: "I'll give you this strawberry if you keep it a secret." -- Matsuda Truly Inspirational.

Production

The two states that produce the most Strawberries are:

  1. California
  2. Florida
Other states that produce strawberries include:
  • New York
  • North Carolina
  • Oregon
  • Washington
The United States is the world's largest producer of strawberries.
